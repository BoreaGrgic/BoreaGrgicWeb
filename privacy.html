Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At BOREA GRGIĆ, accessible from our website, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase digital software products, download software, or contact us for technical support, we may collect personal information necessary to provide the requested service. This may include your name, email address, order information, license information, and information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to process orders, provide software licenses, deliver downloads, and provide technical support.

2. Payment Information and Payment Providers

We do not collect or store customers' credit card numbers or other complete payment card details on our own servers.

Payments for our digital products are processed through a third-party payment and commerce service provider acting as the Merchant of Record. The payment provider processes payment and transaction information in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable data protection laws.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information may be used to:

  • Process and fulfill software orders.
  • Deliver digital software licenses, download links, and license keys.
  • Activate and manage software licenses where applicable.
  • Provide technical support and troubleshoot software-related problems.
  • Communicate important information regarding purchased products, such as security notices, critical updates, or changes affecting the purchased software.
  • Maintain appropriate business, tax, accounting, and transaction records.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized use, and misuse of software licenses.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We may share or make personal information available to third-party service providers where necessary to operate our website, process transactions, deliver software products, provide technical support, or comply with legal obligations.

Such service providers may process personal data only for the purposes necessary to provide their respective services and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential technical cookies or similar technologies required for website functionality, security, navigation, or transaction-related features.

Where third-party services used on our website place cookies or similar technologies, their use may also be subject to the respective third party's privacy and cookie policies.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing support, maintaining license records, resolving disputes, and complying with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

7. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

Where applicable under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate information, deletion of personal data, restriction of processing, objection to certain processing activities, and data portability.

To exercise your rights or ask questions regarding the processing of your personal data, please contact us via our Contact Page.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated modification date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of your personal data, please contact us via our Contact Page.
